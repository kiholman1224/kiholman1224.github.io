Exploration

The solar system is a very interesting place full of unknowns. What we do know about our solar system is that it has eight planets, five dwarf planets, and hundreds of moons.

Start exploring by choosing a group below. Each group contains 4 planets, dwarf planets, or moons, depending on the category. Have fun!

Choose a Category

  • Inner Planets

    Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars: the rocky planets closest to the sun.

  • Outer Planets

    Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune: the gas giants farthest from the sun.

  • Dwarf Planets

    Pluto, Haumea, Ceres, and Eris: the small worlds that orbit the sun but share their neighborhoods with other objects.

  • Moons

    Io, Earth's Moon, Europa, and Titan: four of the most fascinating moon in our solar system
