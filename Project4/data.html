Data

Below are the size, distance, gravity, and average temperature of the planets, dwarf planets, or moons in the exploration tab.

An astronomical unit, which is also known as AU, is the average distance from the Earth to the Sun. It's about 150 million kilometers.

Planets

Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and closest to the Sun, about 4,879 kilometers wide. It's about 0.39 AU from the Sun, has a surface gravity of 3.7 m/s², and an average temperature of 167°C.

Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It's about 12,104 kilometers wide, about 0.72 AU from the Sun, has a surface gravity of 8.9 m/s², and an average temperature of 464°C.

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun. It's about 12,756 kilometers wide, 1 AU from the Sun, has a surface gravity of 9.8 m/s², and an average temperature of 15°C.

Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. It's about 6,792 kilometers wide, about 1.52 AU from the Sun, has a surface gravity of 3.7 m/s², and an average temperature of -65°C.

Jupiter

Jupiter is a gas giant that's the fifth planet from the Sun. It's about 142,984 kilometers wide, about 5.20 AU from the Sun, has a surface gravity of 24.8 m/s², and an average temperature of 112°C.

Saturn

Saturn is a gas giant that's the sixth planet from the Sun. It's about 120,536 kilometers wide, about 9.58 AU from the Sun, has a surface gravity of 10.4 m/s², and an average temperature of -140°C.

Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant that's the seventh planet from the Sun. It's about 51,118 kilometers wide, about 19.2 AU from the Sun, has a surface gravity of 8.9 m/s², and an average temperature of -195°C.

Neptune

Neptune is an ice giant that's the eighth planet from the Sun. It's about 49,528 kilometers wide, about 30.1 AU from the Sun, has a surface gravity of 11.2 m/s², and an average temperature of -200°C.

Dwarf Planets

Pluto

Pluto is a dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt. It's about 2,377 kilometers wide, about 39.5 AU from the Sun and has 5 known moons.

Haumea

Haumea is a dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt. It's about 1,600 kilometers wide on average, because it's oval-shaped due to its rapid rotation. It orbits 43.1 AU from the Sun and has 2 known moons.

Ceres

Ceres is a dwarf planet in the asteroid belt. It's about 940 kilometers wide, about 2.77 AU from the Sun and doesn't have any known moons.

Eris

Eris is a dwarf planet beyond Neptune. It's about 2,326 kilometers wide, about 67.8 AU from the Sun and has 1 known moon.

Moons

Io

Io is a moon of Jupiter. It's about 3,643 kilometers wide and has a surface gravity of 1.8 m/s². It's the most volcanically active object in the Solar System.

Earth's Moon

Earth's Moon is the only natural satellite of Earth. It's about 3,475 kilometers wide and has a surface gravity of 1.62 m/s².

Europa

Europa is a moon of Jupiter. It's about 3,122 kilometers wide and has a surface gravity of 1.31 m/s².

Titan

Titan is a moon of Saturn. It's about 5,150 kilometers wide and has a surface gravity of 1.35 m/s².
